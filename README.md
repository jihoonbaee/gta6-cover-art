Turn a favorite photo and a few words into a custom neon-noir city poster. Sunset Vice Cover Studio runs locally in your browser: photos stay on your device, and the Canvas 2D renderer creates every treatment and export without a build step or runtime network connection.

## Shape the cover

Choose from six photo arrangements: Hero Left, Hero Center, Pop-Out Hero, Strip, Grid 2x2, and Full-Bleed + Insets. Use one photo across all panels for a fast cover or add individual images panel by panel. Smart crop offers a practical starting frame; drag on the artboard, zoom, and switch between fill and fit to get the composition right.

Six color grades set the atmosphere: Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Palm Noir, Golden Hour, Bubblegum, and Mono Pop. Finish the poster with a chrome, outline, or solid title, a subtitle, scene tags, a seal, a callout sticker, billing line, and five-star strip.

## Export

Download a crisp PNG in one click at any of three sizes: square (1080 × 1080), portrait (1080 × 1350), or landscape (1920 × 1080). The live artboard follows the selected format.

## Run locally

With Node.js 22 or later, start the included static server:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in desktop Chrome. The app and bundled fonts work offline after the files are present.
